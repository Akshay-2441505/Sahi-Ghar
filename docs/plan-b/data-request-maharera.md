# Data request to MahaRERA (RTI draft)

Status: **DRAFT for the owner to review, edit and send. Nothing has been sent.** Prepared 2026-09-21.
Why this exists: it was drafted when the owner had decided on official data only. The owner later also approved polite crawling of the open pages (`crawler.md`), so this request is now an **optional second source**: it can still supply what the open pages do not show (PAN, partners, completion status). It is drafted from what the access spike found (`docs/spikes/2026-09-maharera-access.md`); every item below matches a field MahaRERA's own website already shows, so the Authority should hold it.

## 1. What to decide before sending

| Decision | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Who files** | The applicant must give their own name and postal address (and usually phone and email). The online Maharashtra portal is reported to accept **Indian citizens only**. Use your own details; they are left as blanks below. |
| **Route** | (a) Online: `rtionline.maharashtra.gov.in`, if MahaRERA is listed there (third-party guides report that some authorities are not listed; check). (b) Postal or in person to MahaRERA's Public Information Officer (PIO). |
| **PIO name and address** | Third-party guides give: *Public Information Officer, MahaRERA, 9th Floor, Housefin Bhavan, Plot C-21, E-Block, near Kamgar Bhavan, behind RBI Building, Bandra-Kurla Complex, Bandra (East), Mumbai 400051.* **Unverified.** Copy the current details from the "Right to Information" page on MahaRERA's own website (linked from its home page) before sending. |
| **Fee** | Traditionally Rs 10 for the application and a per-page copying charge (guides say Rs 2 per page). One guide says new Maharashtra RTI Rules (2026) raised these to Rs 30 and Rs 5 per page. **Unverified; check the portal or the Authority at filing time.** Pay in the form the route requires. |
| **Scope** | The full request below asks for all of Maharashtra (about 49,000 projects). An Authority may call that a disproportionate diversion of its resources (RTI Act, section 7(9)). The **fallback scope** in item 6 is pre-written so a refusal can be answered quickly. |
| **Personal information** | PAN and partner/director names may be treated as personal information. Item 2 asks for them but says "if withheld, provide the rest". Decide whether you want them in the first request at all. |
| **Purpose** | An RTI application does not need to state a reason. The draft does not give one. The optional letter in section 4 does. |

## 2. The application (full version, for postal filing or an attached annexure)

> **Application under the Right to Information Act, 2005**
>
> To: The Public Information Officer, Maharashtra Real Estate Regulatory Authority (MahaRERA), [address as confirmed on the Authority's website]
>
> From: [Applicant's full name], [postal address], [phone], [email]
>
> Date: [date]
>
> **Subject: Request for an electronic extract of registered-project, promoter, extension and complaint records**
>
> Under section 6(1) of the Right to Information Act, 2005, and section 2(j)(iv) (information in any electronic mode where it is stored in a computer), I request the following information held by the Authority, **in electronic form (CSV or Excel), one file per table**, supplied by email or on a storage device at my cost. Please provide records for all projects registered in Maharashtra from 2017 to the date of extraction, and state the date of extraction.
>
> **1. Registered projects** (one row per project):
> (a) project registration number (P5...); (b) project name; (c) promoter name and the promoter's unique ID or registration number; (d) district, pincode and village/locality as filed; (e) date of registration; (f) end date of the original registration validity; (g) for **each** extension granted: date of the extension certificate and the new "valid up to" date; (h) current registration status (registered, lapsed, revoked, closed or completed); (i) if recorded, the date of the completion certificate or the date the project was marked completed or closed; (j) the date the record was last modified.
>
> **2. Promoters** (one row per promoter):
> (a) promoter unique ID; (b) promoter name; (c) type of promoter (company, LLP, partnership, individual, other); (d) registered office address as filed; (e) PAN; (f) names of the directors, partners or proprietor as filed with the registration. *If the Authority considers (e) or (f) to be personal information exempt under section 8(1)(j), please supply the remaining fields under section 10 (severability) and state the ground for each withheld field.*
>
> **3. Complaints** (one row per complaint, as shown in the Authority's "Promoter Wise Complaint Report" and "Project Wise Complaint Report"):
> (a) complaint number; (b) promoter unique ID and name; (c) project registration number; (d) district; (e) date of filing (if the full date is held) or otherwise year and month; (f) complaint status as recorded; (g) date and type of the latest order or proceeding; (h) whether an application for non-execution or enforcement of the order has been filed, and its status; (i) where recorded, the final outcome (for example allowed, partly allowed, dismissed, withdrawn, settled).
>
> **4. Definitions.** A short note explaining each status value used in the above records (for example "Order Approved", "Roznama Approved", "Hearing Scheduled") and how "extension" and "completion" are recorded.
>
> **5. Format.** One file per table above, with the project registration number and the promoter ID present in every file so the tables can be joined, UTF-8 encoded, dates as DD/MM/YYYY.
>
> **6. If the Authority considers this request too large to supply at once,** please supply items 1 to 5 first for the districts of Mumbai City, Mumbai Suburban, Thane and Pune, and tell me in the reply what the remaining districts would need.
>
> I am willing to pay the prescribed fee and copying charges; please inform me of the amount if it is not already covered. I request a reply within 30 days as provided in section 7(1). If any part is refused, please give the ground and the details of the first appellate authority as required by section 7(8).
>
> [Applicant signature]  [Date]

## 3. The short version (for online forms that limit the text)

Online forms often cap the text length. Paste this and attach the full text above as a PDF annexure if the form allows attachments. Check the portal's actual limit.

> Information sought (electronic form, CSV or Excel, one file per table; all Maharashtra projects registered from 2017 to date; state the extraction date):
> 1. Projects: registration no., name, promoter name and ID, district, pincode, registration date, original registration end date, every extension (certificate date and new valid-up-to date), current status, completion or closure date if recorded, last-modified date.
> 2. Promoters: ID, name, type, registered office address, PAN, directors or partners as filed (if withheld under s.8(1)(j), supply the rest under s.10).
> 3. Complaints: complaint no., promoter ID and name, project registration no., district, filing date (or year and month), status, latest order date and type, whether non-execution was applied for and its status, final outcome if recorded.
> 4. A note defining each status value used.
> Every file to carry project registration no. and promoter ID for joining. If too large for one go, supply Mumbai City, Mumbai Suburban, Thane and Pune first. Prescribed fees will be paid.

## 4. Optional: a letter to the Authority (outside the RTI process)

An RTI gives a one-time extract. A refreshed extract every quarter needs the Authority's agreement, which an RTI cannot compel (it covers information already held, not future information). If you want to ask for that, a short letter to the Chairperson or Secretary can accompany or follow the RTI:

> Sir/Madam, I am building a free, non-commercial public tool that lets homebuyers check a builder's RERA record from official filings before booking. It shows only what the Authority's data says, with a link to the source and the date of the data, and makes no independent judgement. In line with the Authority's Copyright Policy (reproduction with accurate use, no misleading context, and acknowledgement of the source), I request that the Authority share the attached fields as a periodic extract (quarterly if possible), or tell me whether a standing data-sharing arrangement is possible and on what terms. [Name, contact].

## 5. How each item traces to what MahaRERA already shows

| Requested | Where MahaRERA's own site already shows it (spike evidence) |
|---|---|
| Registration no., project name, promoter name, district, pincode, last modified | Project search cards (`spike/samples/*projects-search-result*`) |
| Registration validity start and end | Registration certificate: "valid for a period commencing from ... ending with ..." (`project-1-registration-certificate.txt`) |
| Extension certificate date and new valid-up-to | Extension certificate (`project-5-ext-certificate.pdf`) |
| Promoter registered office | Registration certificate text |
| Complaint fields, statuses, non-execution flags | Promoter complaint detail table (`*promoter-complaint-view-data*`) |
| PAN, partners/directors, completion status and date, promoter type | **Not on any open page**; they sit in the project detail view behind a CAPTCHA, so they are the fields most worth asking for and the ones the Authority may withhold. |

## 6. Timeline and follow-up

| Step | When | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Reply due | 30 days from receipt (RTI Act s.7(1)) | Note the date it is received. |
| First appeal | Within 30 days of the reply or of the deadline passing (check the current rule and the appellate authority named in the reply) | Decided within 30 days, extendable to 45 (s.19). |
| Second appeal | To the State Information Commission if the first appeal fails | Only if worth the effort. |

Keep a note of: date sent, route, fee paid and how, reference or registration number, date the reply arrived.

## 7. What happens when the data arrives

The file-import adapter (next in Plan B) will read exactly these tables. It needs no crawler, and each imported file becomes a `source_document` so every number on a trust page links back to "MahaRERA data extract, dated <date>". If the Authority supplies different columns or formats, the adapter's parser is adjusted to match; the rest of the system does not change.

## 8. Other states
